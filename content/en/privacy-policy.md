## 1. Data Controller

The controller responsible for the processing of any data that may be collected on this website is Roberto Morais (fuenrob), owner of the domain [https://fuenrob.com](https://fuenrob.com).

## 2. Data Collection and Purpose

This website is a personal static blog and **does not directly collect or store visitors' personal data**.

* **Forms:** There are no registration forms or personal data collection.
* **Bulletins / Newsletters:** No bulk emails are sent and no email subscriptions are requested.
* **Comments:** The site does not have its own comment storage system.

## 3. Cookies and Analytics

This website is hosted on static infrastructure (GitHub Pages) and prioritizes respect for user privacy. No advertising or third-party tracking cookies are used for profiling purposes.

Should privacy-friendly analytics services be used in the future, they will only collect aggregated, anonymous metrics without storing full IP addresses or individually identifying users.

For any questions regarding privacy or data processing on this blog, you can get in touch through the social media profiles linked on the website.
